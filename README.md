# AQUAVERA — Aquarium & Aquascaping

> **Build an underwater world, not just an aquarium.**

AQUAVERA is a modern aquarium and aquascaping storefront concept built around one idea: an aquarium should feel like a living environment, not a collection of equipment.

The store brings together aquarium accessories, aquascaping essentials, filtration, lighting, water-care tools, maintenance equipment, hardscape elements, and display-focused products in one curated experience.

---

## 🌊 The AQUAVERA Concept

Most aquarium stores organize products around technical categories.

**AQUAVERA starts somewhere else.**

It starts with the environment the customer wants to create.

Instead of asking:

> “What product do I need?”

AQUAVERA encourages visitors to think:

> “What kind of underwater world do I want to build?”

From there, the storefront guides the customer toward the equipment, tools, materials, and accessories that support that vision.

### The experience is organized around four layers

**01 — BUILD**  
Substrate, rocks, driftwood, tools, plants, and aquascaping essentials.

**02 — BALANCE**  
Filtration, water movement, aeration, heating, testing, and water-care products.

**03 — ILLUMINATE**  
Aquarium lighting, timers, plant-focused illumination, and visual enhancement.

**04 — MAINTAIN**  
Cleaning equipment, replacement accessories, maintenance tools, and everyday aquarium care.

# 🎨 Visual Direction

AQUAVERA uses a visual system inspired by natural underwater environments.

### Primary visual references

- Deep water
- Aquatic greens
- Clear glass
- Natural stone
- Driftwood
- Plant textures
- Soft reflections
- Controlled highlights

### Design principles

**Depth**  
Dark backgrounds create the feeling of looking through aquarium glass.

**Life**  
Green vegetation and aquatic imagery keep the experience organic.

**Clarity**  
Product information remains clean and readable.

**Contrast**  
Bright aquatic accents guide attention without overwhelming the page.

**Consistency**  
Product photography should use clean, centered compositions and predictable framing.

---

# 📸 Image Standard

Product imagery is an important part of the AQUAVERA experience.

Images should be:

- Realistic
- Clean
- High quality
- Correctly framed
- Centered when appropriate
- Consistent in aspect ratio
- Free from website screenshots
- Free from prices, buttons, ratings, or interface elements
- Focused on the actual product or aquarium environment

For product grids, square or near-square imagery is preferred.

For hero sections, wide environmental photography can be used to establish atmosphere.
